General strategy

  • You hear each recording twice — use the first listening for the overall gist, the second to confirm details.
  • Read the questions before listening — they tell you exactly what to listen for.
  • In Part 2, predict the type of word missing (a number? a name? a place?) before you hear it.
  • Watch for distractors: speakers often mention one thing, then correct themselves — "I was going to say Tuesday, but actually it's Wednesday" — the exam tests the corrected information.
  • Don't panic if you miss a word — keep listening. You can often work it out from context on the second listen.
